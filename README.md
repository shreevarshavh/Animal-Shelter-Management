# PawCare — Animal Shelter Management System

A digital platform for managing animal shelter records, medical histories, daily care, shelter capacity, and adoption processes.

---

## Overview

Animal shelters often rely on paper registers and spreadsheets to maintain animal records. PawCare aims to simplify shelter operations by providing a centralized system for recording animal information, tracking medical care, managing shelter capacity, and organizing adoptions.

---

## Features

- Animal Registration: Register rescued, abandoned, and surrendered animals with unique IDs, photographs, and intake details.

- Digital Animal Records: Maintain animal profiles, identification details, rescue history, and current status.

- Medical Records: Track vaccinations, illnesses, injuries, treatments, medications, and veterinary notes.

- Shelter Capacity: Manage kennel assignments and monitor available shelter spaces.

- Dashboard: View animal statistics, medical alerts, adoption availability, and shelter occupancy.
